## ZSFTP / PS5 ZeroSpace FTP

ZeroSpace's Transfer module is designed around the ZSFTP engine developed in the PS5 ZeroSpace FTP project, itself derived from the original `rarftp` project by Rui Nelson.

When ZSFTP source and dependencies are vendored here, preserve their applicable copyright notices and license texts alongside distributed binaries.

No GPL-licensed ps5upload source is included in this foundation. Similar future functionality should be implemented independently unless ZeroSpace's licensing strategy is deliberately changed.
